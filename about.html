About BioCAD Resources

Learn Better. Build Smarter. Grow Faster.

Our Mission

BioCAD Resources exists to help engineers, students, and entrepreneurs learn practical technology skills that get results. No fluff. No theory that doesn’t work in the real world.

We focus on 4 areas that matter in 2026: Engineering, CAD, AI, and Digital Business.

Who We Are

We are a team of engineers, CAD trainers, and web developers based in Nigeria. We’ve built products, designed parts in Autodesk Inventor, coded websites with HTML/CSS/JS, and used AI to work faster.

We got tired of seeing students struggle with outdated tutorials and expensive software. So we started BioCAD Resources to bridge that gap.

What We Do

  • ✅ Educate: Step-by-step guides, tutorials, and buying guides for CAD, AI, and Web Dev
  • ✅ Train: Practical CAD and website development training for students and professionals
  • ✅ Recommend: Honest reviews of laptops, software, and tools engineers actually need
  • ✅ Build: Website development services for engineers and small businesses

Our Core Belief

"Technology is only useful when you can apply it. We teach you how to build, not just how to watch."
